Project 01 · Data Analytics · Europe

Digital Europe. Evidence.

An end-to-end data analytics case study examining the relationship between economic development and technology adoption across European countries.

Project status

Completed case study

Data sourcing · validation · SQL · statistical analysis · visualization · business interpretation

01 / Overview

From public data to business evidence.

Understanding how digitalisation and economic development move together across Europe.

This project investigates differences in technology adoption across European countries and examines the relationships that can be observed between digitalisation and economic development.

The analysis follows a complete analytical workflow: defining the business question, sourcing public data, validating and preparing the data, analysing it with SQL and statistical methods, creating visualisations, and translating the results into business-oriented findings.
