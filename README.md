# Project 01 — Digital Europe

## Exploring the Relationship Between Economic Development and Technology Adoption

**Live case study:**  
https://sinosk31-a11y.github.io/projects/project-01/

### Business Question

How does technology adoption vary across European countries, and what relationships can be observed between digitalization and economic development?

### Research Questions

The project investigates:

1. How does GDP per capita vary across European countries?
2. How does the prevalence of digital skills differ between countries?
3. How widely are enterprises adopting artificial intelligence?
4. How widely are enterprises adopting cloud computing?
5. How does the share of ICT specialists differ across countries?
6. What relationships can be observed between GDP per capita and technology-adoption indicators?
7. How have selected digital indicators changed between comparable years and 2025?
